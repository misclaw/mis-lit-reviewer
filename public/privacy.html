Privacy

Paper Trails & Paper Trails Bridge · Effective 2026-07-20

Paper Trails is a literature-review workbench for Information Systems scholars. This page covers the web app (mis-lit-reviewer.misclaw.app), the Mac app, and the Paper Trails Bridge browser extension.

The browser extension collects nothing

Paper Trails Bridge has no server, no analytics, and no storage. When you click its button on a supported search site (Google Scholar, Asta, Paper Digest, SciSpace), it reads the paper list visible on that page and opens Paper Trails with those results encoded in the URL fragment. URL fragments are processed inside your browser and are never transmitted over the network, so the results travel directly from one of your tabs to another. The extension does nothing until you click it, and it sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

Your research data stays with you

By default, everything you do in Paper Trails — research questions, review streams, imported sessions, preferences — is stored only in your browser (localStorage). We run no server that receives it.

If you create an optional sync account, that same workspace is stored in a private database row (hosted on Supabase) that only your authenticated account can read or write, so you can use Paper Trails on multiple devices. You can stop syncing at any time by signing out; your local copy stays with you. To delete the cloud copy, contact us (below).

API keys never leave your browser

LLM API keys you add (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) are stored only in your browser and sent only to the provider they belong to, with your requests. They are excluded from account sync and from data exports.

AI requests

When you run a review or web search, your research question and researcher profile are sent directly from your browser to the LLM provider you chose, using your own key, under that provider's privacy terms. We are not in that path.

Analytics

Production pages of the web app load Google Analytics for anonymous, aggregate page-view statistics. No research content, workspace data, or API keys are sent to analytics. The browser extension contains no analytics.
